[h1]About[/h1]
Empire traders and quests never give you the persona you want. Now you can close the gap: at the fabrication bench, with research gates, component costs, and balance settings that keep it feeling earned.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Trait Customization[/h2]
[list]
[*]Add and remove bladelink traits at the fabrication bench
[*]Convert a base monosword/plasmasword/zeushammer into its persona variant by adding its first trait, installing an AI persona core
[*]Remove all traits to revert a persona weapon back to its base form, refunding the core
[*]Full vanilla validation: max trait count, exclusion-tag conflicts, and sole-trait restrictions are all enforced
[/list]

[h2]Reprogramming Costs[/h2]
Bladelink traits are facets of the persona's personality, not bolt-on parts. Changing them means forcefully reprogramming the persona with advanced components that burn out in the process.
[list]
[*]First trait / last trait costs or refunds exactly one AI persona core
[*]Every other change costs advanced components in both directions - additions and removals - reprogramming is destructive, there are no refunds
[*]Component count scales with weapon quality above a configurable threshold; the settings page shows a live cost table
[*]Real-time cost preview before you commit
[/list]

[h2]Rename & Recolor[/h2]
[list]
[*]Rename the persona using vanilla's bladelink namer, or type your own
[*]Recolor via the default persona tint plus Ideology and structure palettes
[*]Ideology relic names stay locked and can only be changed through ideology reform, as intended
[/list]

[h2]Three Ways to Customize[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Weapon gizmo:[/b] select a weapon, click "Customize persona", and pick a colonist
[*][b]Workbench menu:[/b] select a colonist and right-click a fabrication bench to customize their equipped or carried weapons
[*][b]Ground weapon menu:[/b] right-click a weapon on the ground to send a colonist to the nearest operational fabrication bench
[/list]

[h2]Research & Crafting[/h2]
A single ultratech research project, Bladelink Customization, gates everything below - it requires Advanced Fabrication and (by default) one Empire-sourced techprint.
[list]
[*]No workbench tiers to worry about - persona weapons are all ultratech, so customization is static to the fabrication bench (or a VEF-recognized equivalent)
[*]Craftable base monosword, plasmasword, and zeushammer recipes at the fabrication bench, each individually toggleable - craft the base weapon, then install a persona core for a fully in-colony bespoke persona weapon
[*]Techprint requirement is a live-applied slider (0-3); setting it to 0 removes the requirement entirely, no restart needed
[/list]

[h2]Optional Trait Discovery[/h2]
[list]
[*]Restrict available traits to those seen on persona weapons held by the colony, in a caravan, or on hostiles - no persistent save state
[*]Hostile-held sources show in the list but can't be added until the weapon is captured
[/list]

[h1]Mod Compatibility[/h1]
[list]
[*]Requires the Royalty DLC
[*]Safe to add to existing saves

[h1]FAQ[/h1]
[b]Q: Do I need the Royalty DLC?[/b]
A: Yes. This mod builds on Royalty's bladelink weapon system.

[b]Q: Do I need Odyssey too?[/b]
A: No. This mod is Royalty-only. If you also want to customize Odyssey's unique weapons, check out the sibling mod Unique Weapons Unbound - the two coexist without conflict.

[b]Q: Why can't I customize [weapon name]?[/b]
A: Only weapons with a resolvable base/persona pairing via vanilla's bladelink system are supported. The three vanilla persona weapons (monosword, plasmasword, zeushammer) work out of the box; modded persona weapons work automatically if they use CompBladelinkWeapon.

[b]Q: Can I customize weapons from other mods?[/b]
A: If the mod adds persona weapons using vanilla's CompBladelinkWeapon system, they're automatically supported, including their own traits and namers.

[b]Q: Is this imbalanced?[/b]
A: That's up to you. The default settings require research, a techprint, and meaningful component costs. If you want it harder, raise the component costs. If you want it easier, wind them down or skip the research entirely. Your colony, your rules.

[b]Q: Can I interrupt a customization job safely?[/b]
A: Yes. Each trait or cosmetic change is a separate crafting job. Interrupting won't lose resources; unfinished work is simply cancelled.

[b]Q: What happens to a bonded weapon's bond?[/b]
A: Adding the free-wielding trait or reverting a bonded persona weapon to its base form severs the bond - both cases show a confirmation warning first.
